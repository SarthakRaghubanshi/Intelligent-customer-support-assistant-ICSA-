=========================================
PICKUP SETTINGS: BURGERS & CO
=========================================

1. PICKUP LOCATIONS & CURBSIDE SPOTS
------------------------------------
Burgers & Co offers two convenient ways to collect your pickup order at Plot 44, Block C, Sector 62, Noida:
* In-Store Counter: Walk in and proceed to the designated "Online Order Pickup" counter. Have your order ID ready.
* Curbside Pickup: Park in one of our designated curbside pickup spaces (marked spots 1-5). Use the app or call the support line to notify us of your arrival, and we will bring the order to your vehicle.

2. HOLDING POLICY
-----------------
* Food Quality Window:
  - To maintain structural integrity and heat, our burgers are held in heated holding drawers for a maximum of 1.5 hours.
  - If not collected within 1.5 hours, the order is discarded for food safety reasons, and the customer is not eligible for a refund.

3. PRE-ORDERING & SCHEDULED PICKUPS
------------------------------------
* Customers can schedule a pickup time up to 7 days in advance.
* Cancellations for scheduled pickups are eligible for a full refund up to 1 hour before the scheduled preparation time.
