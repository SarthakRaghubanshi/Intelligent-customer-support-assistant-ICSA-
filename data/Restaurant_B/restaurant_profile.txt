Brand & Quality Promise:
------------------------
At Burgers & Co, we believe a great burger starts with superior ingredients. Our signature patties are made from 100% fresh, never-frozen ground beef, smashed on a 450°F flat-top grill to create a beautiful caramelized lace crust while locking in the juices. We bake our soft brioche buns daily, and all sauces are house-made from secret family recipes. We maintain a separate grill section and clean utensils for cooking our vegan/plant-based patties to ensure strict separation for our vegetarian and vegan customers.
