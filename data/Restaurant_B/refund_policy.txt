=========================================
REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY: BURGERS & CO
=========================================

1. CANCELLATION RULES
---------------------
* Immediate Cancellations (Within 10 Minutes):
  - Customers can cancel their order for a full refund within 10 minutes of placing it. This can be done directly through the website/app order tracking page or by calling +91 98765 55555.
* Late Cancellations (After 10 Minutes):
  - Because our burgers are freshly grilled, preparation begins soon after ordering. If you cancel an order more than 10 minutes after placement, a 50% cancellation fee will apply to cover the ingredient costs. The remaining 50% will be refunded to your original payment method.
* Scheduled/Advance Orders:
  - Orders scheduled for a future pickup or delivery time can be cancelled for a full refund up to 1 hour before the scheduled preparation start time.

2. REFUND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
------------------------------
We strive for perfection, but if we make a mistake, we want to correct it. Refund eligibility is determined based on the following:

* Missing or Incorrect Items:
  - If an item is missing from your order or you receive the wrong item (e.g., chicken instead of beef), you are eligible for a 100% refund for that specific item, OR we will redeliver/remake the correct item immediately at no extra cost.
* Quality Issues (Cold Food / Damaged Packaging):
  - If your burger arrives cold or the packaging is severely damaged, you are eligible for a full refund of the affected items or a complete order replacement.
  - To claim a refund for quality issues, customers must report the issue within 1 hour of delivery/pickup and provide a photo of the affected food/packaging to support@burgersandco.com or upload it via the live chat.
* Uncollected Pickup Orders:
  - Pickup orders are held in our heated drawers for a maximum of 1.5 hours. If an order is not collected within 1.5 hours of the scheduled pickup time, the food will be discarded for food safety reasons, and the customer will be charged the full amount of the order. No refunds are available for uncollected orders.
* Delivery Issues (Wrong Address / No-Show):
  - If a driver cannot reach a customer (e.g., wrong phone number, no gate code provided, no answer at door) after attempting contact for 10 minutes, the driver will return the order to the restaurant. The customer will be charged a 40% restocking/food wastage fee + the full delivery fee.

3. REFUND PROCESSING TIMES
---------------------------
Refunds are processed to the original payment method. The timelines are as follows:
* Credit/Debit Card Payments:
  - Refund requests are processed by our system immediately upon approval. However, the funds may take 3 to 5 business days to appear in your bank account, depending on your financial institution.
* Digital Wallets (Apple Pay / Google Pay):
  - Reflected in your digital wallet balance or linked card within 24 to 48 hours.
* Burgers & Co Store Credit:
  - Issued instantly and available for immediate use on your next order.
