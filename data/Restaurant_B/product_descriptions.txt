SIGNATURE BURGERS
-----------------
* Classic Smash Burger
  - Description: Our signature burger made with a 100% fresh ground beef patty smashed thin on a sizzling flat-top grill to develop a crispy, caramelized lace edge. Placed inside a toasted buttered brioche bun with a slice of melted American cheese, dill pickles, shredded lettuce, and custom house burger sauce.
  - Taste Profile: Juicy, savory beef balanced with tangy pickles and sweet, creamy house sauce.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Milk (Dairy), Eggs, Soy.

* Bacon Cheddar Crunch
  - Description: A rich and smoky burger featuring our signature smashed patty, topped with crispy applewood-smoked bacon slices, melted sharp cheddar cheese, beer-battered crispy fried onion strings, and a generous drizzle of house smoky BBQ sauce.
  - Taste Profile: Savory, smoky, sweet, and crunchy.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Milk (Dairy), Soy.

* Crunchy Veggie Delite
  - Description: A crispy breaded vegetable and potato patty seasoned with fine herbs, fried golden-brown. Served on a toasted sesame bun with melted American cheese, fresh red onion slices, crisp leaf lettuce, and sweet chili mayo.
  - Taste Profile: Mildly spiced, crunchy, fresh, and creamy.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Milk (Dairy), Eggs.
