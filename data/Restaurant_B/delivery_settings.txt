=========================================
DELIVERY SETTINGS: BURGERS & CO
=========================================

1. DELIVERY RADIUS & ZONES
--------------------------
Burgers & Co operates a dedicated internal delivery fleet. We deliver up to a maximum radius of 8.0 kilometers from our kitchen at Plot 44, Block C, Sector 62, Noida. The delivery area is divided into three zones:

* Zone 1 (Near): Up to 2.5 kilometers radius
* Zone 2 (Mid): 2.6 to 5.0 kilometers radius
* Zone 3 (Far): 5.1 to 8.0 kilometers radius

*Deliveries requested outside the 8.0-kilometer radius are not accepted by our internal fleet but may be ordered via partner platforms (e.g., Zomato, Swiggy) if within their respective service boundaries.*

2. DELIVERY CHARGES
-------------------
Delivery charges are structured by zone to cover driver fuel and maintenance:
* Zone 1 (0.0 - 2.5 km):
  - Delivery Fee: ₹79
  - Free Delivery: For orders over ₹599 (subtotal before taxes and fees)
* Zone 2 (2.6 - 5.0 km):
  - Delivery Fee: ₹119
  - Free Delivery: For orders over ₹899 (subtotal before taxes and fees)
* Zone 3 (5.1 - 8.0 km):
  - Delivery Fee: ₹159
  - Free Delivery: For orders over ₹1299 (subtotal before taxes and fees)

*Minimum Order Subtotal for Delivery: ₹200 (excluding taxes and delivery fee).*

3. ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIMES (EDT)
---------------------------------
Delivery times vary depending on order volume, traffic, weather, and distance:
* Zone 1 (0.0 - 2.5 km):
  - Standard Hours: 20 - 30 minutes
  - Peak Hours (Fri-Sat, 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM): 35 - 45 minutes
* Zone 2 (2.6 - 5.0 km):
  - Standard Hours: 30 - 40 minutes
  - Peak Hours (Fri-Sat, 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM): 45 - 55 minutes
* Zone 3 (5.1 - 8.0 km):
  - Standard Hours: 40 - 50 minutes
  - Peak Hours (Fri-Sat, 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM): 55 - 65 minutes

*Weather Advisory: During heavy rain or extreme weather, add an additional 15 minutes to all zones for driver safety.*
