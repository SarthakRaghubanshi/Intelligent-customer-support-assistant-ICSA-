=========================================
DELIVERY POLICY: BURGERS & CO
=========================================

1. SERVICES & boundaries
------------------------
* Burgers & Co provides internal delivery service within an 8.0-kilometer radius of our kitchen at Plot 44, Block C, Sector 62, Noida.
* Address Verification:
  - Customers must verify that their delivery address is within our 8.0 km radius prior to finalizing orders. Address lookup is automated in our app and website.
  - Deliveries requested outside our boundary will be blocked.

2. ORDER WAITING LIMITS & RE-DELIVERY
--------------------------------------
* Driver Waiting Limit:
  - When the driver arrives at the customer's location, they will attempt to contact the customer via call/text. The driver is authorized to wait for a maximum of 10 minutes.
  - If the customer does not respond within 10 minutes, the driver will return the order to the restaurant.
* Re-delivery/Restocking Fee:
  - Unclaimed returned orders will incur a 40% food wastage and restocking fee.
  - Re-delivery requests will require a new delivery fee and the customer must pay for the remade items.

3. WEATHER & PEAK-HOUR SAFETY
------------------------------
* We prioritize driver safety. In cases of torrential rain or waterlogging, delivery times may be extended.
* Customers will receive SMS updates if delays exceed 15 minutes past the estimated delivery time.
