==================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ): BURGERS & CO
==================================================

Q1: What are your operating hours?
A1: Burgers & Co is open Monday through Thursday from 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM, Friday and Saturday from 12:00 PM to 01:00 AM (for late-night cravings), and Sunday from 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM. The kitchen closes 30 minutes before the restaurant closes.

Q2: What is your delivery radius and charges?
A2: We deliver within a maximum radius of 8.0 kilometers from our kitchen at Sector 62, Noida. Delivery fees are:
- Zone 1 (up to 2.5 km): ₹79 (free on orders over ₹599)
- Zone 2 (2.6 - 5.0 km): ₹119 (free on orders over ₹899)
- Zone 3 (5.1 - 8.0 km): ₹159 (free on orders over ₹1299)

Q3: What is the cancellation window for order refunds?
A3: You can cancel your order for a full refund within 10 minutes of placing it. Any cancellations made after 10 minutes will incur a 50% cancellation fee.

Q4: Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?
A4: Yes! We offer the Crunchy Veggie Delite burger for vegetarians and the plant-based Vegan BBQ Char burger. We also offer dairy-free vegan cheddar cheese substitutions and gluten-free buns.
