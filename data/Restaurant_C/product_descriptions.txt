SUSHI & RAMEN ITEMS
-------------------
* Salmon Nigiri
  - Description: Premium fresh Norwegian salmon sliced thin and placed over hand-formed pods of warm seasoned short-grain sushi rice. Served with a smear of real wasabi beneath the fish and a side of soy sauce and pickled ginger.
  - Taste Profile: Rich, buttery, clean seafood flavor balanced by sweet-acidic seasoned rice and sharp wasabi.
  - Allergens: Fish, Soy.

* Spicy Tuna Roll
  - Description: An 8-piece sushi roll filled with minced yellowfin tuna mixed with hot sriracha mayo, crisp cucumber matchsticks, and green onions. Rolled in toasted sesame seeds.
  - Taste Profile: Spiced, creamy, crunchy, and savory.
  - Allergens: Fish, Soy, Sesame, Eggs.

* Zen Tonkotsu Ramen
  - Description: An authentic ramen consisting of thin, straight wheat noodles served in our signature rich pork-bone broth, simmered for 18 hours to release rich gelatin and marrow. Topped with pan-seared pork belly slices, a soft-boiled egg marinated in soy-mirin, seasoned wood-ear mushrooms, bamboo shoots, and a sheet of toasted nori.
  - Taste Profile: Hearty, deep, complex umami flavor with creamy broth.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Eggs, Soy.
