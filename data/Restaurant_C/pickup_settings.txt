=========================================
PICKUP SETTINGS: SUSHI ZEN
=========================================

1. PICKUP LOCATIONS
--------------------
Sushi Zen offers pickup collection at Plot 88, Sector 15, Noida:
* In-Store Counter: Walk in and proceed to the designated "Sushi pickup" counter. Have your order ID ready.

2. HOLDING POLICY
-----------------
* Food Quality Window:
  - To maintain raw fish safety and temperature, our sushi is held in chilled display cases for a maximum of 1 hour.
  - If not collected within 1 hour, the order is discarded for food safety reasons, and the customer is not eligible for a refund.

3. PRE-ORDERING & SCHEDULED PICKUPS
------------------------------------
* Customers can schedule a pickup time up to 7 days in advance.
* Cancellations for scheduled pickups are eligible for a full refund up to 2 hours before the scheduled preparation time.
