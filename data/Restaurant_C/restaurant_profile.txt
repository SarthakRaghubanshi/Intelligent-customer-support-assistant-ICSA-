Brand & Quality Promise:
------------------------
At Sushi Zen, we are dedicated to presenting the finest culinary art of Japan. We source our seafood daily from premium global markets, ensuring Sashimi-grade quality and freshness. Our seasoned sushi rice is made with authentic Japanese short-grain rice, seasoned with custom-brewed vinegar. Our hot broths for Tonkotsu ramen are simmered for a full 18 hours to build deep umami profiles. Due to the delicate nature of raw seafood and heat-sensitive broths, we maintain a climate-controlled kitchen and package all deliveries in insulated boxes with cold packs.
