==================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ): SUSHI ZEN
==================================================

Q1: What are your operating hours?
A1: Sushi Zen operates on split shifts from Monday through Thursday: Lunch is from 12:00 PM to 03:00 PM, and Dinner is from 06:00 PM to 10:30 PM. On Friday and Saturday, we serve continuously from 12:00 PM to 11:30 PM. We are closed all day Sunday for deep sanitization.

Q2: What is your delivery radius and charges?
A2: We deliver up to a maximum radius of 12.0 kilometers from our kitchen at Sector 15, Noida, using climate-controlled packaging. Delivery fees are:
- Zone 1 (up to 4.0 km): ₹129 (free on orders over ₹999)
- Zone 2 (4.1 - 8.0 km): ₹179 (free on orders over ₹1499)
- Zone 3 (8.1 - 12.0 km): ₹229 (free on orders over ₹1999)

Q3: What is the cancellation window for order refunds?
A3: Due to the delicate preparation of raw fish, orders can only be cancelled for a full refund within 3 minutes of placement. Cancellations after 3 minutes incur a 100% cancellation fee.

Q4: Do you accept Cash on Delivery?
A4: No. Sushi Zen does not accept Cash on Delivery (COD). All orders must be prepaid online.
