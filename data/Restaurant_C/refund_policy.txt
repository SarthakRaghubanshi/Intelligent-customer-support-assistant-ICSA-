=========================================
REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY: SUSHI ZEN
=========================================

1. CANCELLATION RULES
---------------------
* Immediate Cancellations (Within 3 Minutes):
  - Due to the rapid and delicate preparation of raw fish, orders can only be cancelled for a full refund within 3 minutes of placement. This can be done by calling +91 98765 66666.
* Late Cancellations (After 3 Minutes):
  - Once prep has begun, raw seafood cannot be restocked or reused. Cancellations made more than 3 minutes after placement will incur a 100% cancellation fee. No refunds will be provided.
* Scheduled/Advance Orders:
  - Orders scheduled for a future pickup or delivery time can be cancelled for a full refund up to 2 hours before the scheduled preparation start time.

2. REFUND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
------------------------------
Due to food safety standards governing raw seafood, refund criteria are strictly enforced:

* Missing or Incorrect Items:
  - If an item is missing or you receive the wrong item, you are eligible for a 100% refund for that specific item.
* Quality Issues (Temp Control / Spoilage):
  - If your sushi arrives warm or is visually damaged, you are eligible for a refund of the affected items.
  - To claim a refund for quality issues, customers must report the issue within 30 minutes of delivery/pickup and provide a photo of the affected food to support@sushizen.com or upload it via live chat.
* Uncollected Pickup Orders:
  - Pickup orders containing raw fish are held in our chilled display cases for a maximum of 1 hour. If an order is not collected within 1 hour, the food will be discarded for health reasons, and the customer will be charged the full amount. No refunds are available for uncollected orders.
* Delivery Issues (Wrong Address / No-Show):
  - If a driver cannot reach a customer after attempting contact for 5 minutes, the driver will return the order to the restaurant. The customer will be charged a 100% restocking/food wastage fee + the full delivery fee.

3. REFUND PROCESSING TIMES
---------------------------
Refunds are processed to the original payment method. The timelines are as follows:
* Credit/Debit Card/UPI Payments:
  - Refund requests are processed by our system immediately upon approval. However, the funds may take 3 to 5 business days to appear in your bank account, depending on your financial institution.
* Digital Wallets (Apple Pay / Google Pay):
  - Reflected in your digital wallet balance or linked card within 24 to 48 hours.
* Sushi Zen Store Credit:
  - Issued instantly and available for immediate use on your next order.
