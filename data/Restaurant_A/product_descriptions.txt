VEGETARIAN PIZZAS
-----------------
* Margherita Royale
  - Description: The quintessential Italian classic, elevated. Hand-stretched 48-hour fermented sourdough crust topped with rich, bright San Marzano tomato sauce, slices of premium fresh mozzarella cheese, aromatic whole fresh basil leaves, and finished with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.
  - Taste Profile: Crispy, chewy crust with a sweet, acidic tomato balance, creamy mozzarella, and clean, fresh herbal notes.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Milk (Dairy).
  - Preparation: Baked in our 900°F wood-fired brick oven.

* Four Cheese Garden
  - Description: A luxurious white pizza base layered with four premium cheeses: shredded mozzarella, sharp gorgonzola blue cheese, shaved parmesan, and dollops of creamy ricotta cheese. Topped with sweet cherry tomatoes, fresh baby spinach, and a touch of roasted garlic.
  - Taste Profile: Rich, creamy, and mildly pungent from the gorgonzola, balanced by sweet cherry tomatoes and earthy spinach.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Milk (Dairy).
  - Preparation: Baked in our wood-fired brick oven.

* Truffle Mushroom
  - Description: A gourmet vegetarian masterpiece with a white cream base, loaded with pan-roasted wild cremini and shiitake mushrooms, seasoned with fresh rosemary, melted fontina cheese, and drizzled with premium white truffle oil after baking.
  - Taste Profile: Deeply earthy, woodsy, and aromatic, with a buttery fontina cheese melt and a rich truffle oil finish.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Milk (Dairy).
  - Preparation: Baked in our wood-fired brick oven.

VEGAN PIZZAS
------------
* Vegan Garden Party
  - Description: A colorful medley of fresh, oven-roasted vegetables including sweet bell peppers, tender artichoke hearts, sliced red onions, and rich Kalamata olives. Served on our sourdough crust with San Marzano tomato sauce and melted dairy-free vegan mozzarella.
  - Taste Profile: Savory, tangy, and garden-fresh, with a subtle olive brine and sweet roasted pepper note.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten. (100% Dairy-Free/Vegan).
  - Preparation: Prepared on dedicated vegan prep surfaces with separate utensils.

* Spicy Vegan Sausage
  - Description: A bold plant-based creation featuring house-made vegan Italian sausage crumbles (soy/pea protein base), fresh jalapeño slices, and vegan mozzarella on top of a fiery spicy marinara sauce, garnished with cold fresh arugula leaves post-bake.
  - Taste Profile: Hot, peppery, and savory, with a distinct anise-and-fennel note from the vegan sausage and a peppery bite from fresh arugula.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten, Soy. (100% Dairy-Free/Vegan).
  - Preparation: Prepared on dedicated vegan prep surfaces.

* Vegan Avocado Dream
  - Description: A cream-free white pizza with a garlic-infused olive oil base, topped with baby spinach, sweet cherry tomatoes, and vegan mozzarella. Immediately after baking, it is layered with fresh sliced avocado and finished with a zigzag drizzle of creamy vegan garlic ai drizzle.
  - Taste Profile: Fresh, rich, and creamy from the avocado and aioli, with a sweet burst of cherry tomatoes and a sharp garlic undertone.
  - Allergens: Wheat/Gluten. (100% Dairy-Free/Vegan).
  - Preparation: Prepared on dedicated vegan prep surfaces.
