=========================================
DELIVERY POLICY: PIZZA PARADISE
=========================================

1. DELIVERY PROTOCOLS
---------------------
* Contactless Delivery: Enabled by default for all online pre-paid orders. Our drivers will place the insulated pizza carrier bag on a clean platform, retrieve your boxed items, place them on your doorstep, knock or ring the bell, and step back at least 6 feet. They will wait to verify that you have collected the food. If you live in an apartment building with lobby-only access, please specify in the delivery notes whether the driver should leave the order at the front desk/concierge or call you upon arrival.
* Direct Hand-off: Available upon request. If you select cash on delivery (COD), direct hand-off is mandatory.
* Gated Communities / High-rise Buildings: Customers are required to provide gate codes or security clearance instructions in the "Delivery Instructions" field at checkout. If the driver is denied entry or cannot reach the customer within 5 minutes of arrival, they will return the order to the restaurant (refer to the refund policy for details).

2. LATE DELIVERIES & GUARANTEES
--------------------------------
* 45-Minute Guarantee (Zone 1 Only): We guarantee that Zone 1 orders will arrive within 45 minutes of the order confirmation timestamp. If a Zone 1 delivery takes longer than 45 minutes (excluding cases of extreme weather or inaccurate customer addresses), the customer is eligible for a ₹250 Pizza Paradise digital voucher redeemable on their next order.
* Standard Delay Policy (Zones 2 & 3): If an order is delayed by more than 20 minutes past the maximum estimated delivery time (EDT) communicated at checkout, the customer will receive an automatic refund of their delivery fee (₹79 or ₹99) and a 15% discount code for their next order.
* Service Disruption: In rare circumstances of severe weather, waterlogging, or peak order volumes where delivery times exceed 90 minutes, Pizza Paradise reserves the right to temporarily suspend new delivery orders or switch to "Pickup Only" mode. Active orders impacted by such suspensions will be cancelled, and full refunds will be issued immediately.

3. THIRD-PARTY PLATFORM DELIVERIES
----------------------------------
* Pizza Paradise partner platforms include Zomato and Swiggy.
* Orders placed on third-party platforms are subject to the pricing, delivery fees, and service terms set by those platforms.
* Issue Resolution: If you ordered through a third-party app and experience a missing item, cold food, or late delivery, you MUST request support directly through that third-party platform's help center. Pizza Paradise staff cannot process refunds, cancel orders, or modify delivery instructions for orders placed through third-party applications.
* In-House Fleet Preference: We encourage ordering directly through our website (pizzaparadise.com) or mobile app to receive lower menu prices, direct support, and participation in our customer loyalty program.
