=========================================
DELIVERY SETTINGS: PIZZA PARADISE
=========================================

1. DELIVERY RADIUS & ZONES
--------------------------
Pizza Paradise operates its own fleet of delivery drivers. We deliver to a maximum radius of 10.0 kilometers from our kitchen at Plot 15, Block K, Sector 18, Noida. The delivery area is divided into three zones:

* Zone 1 (Near): Up to 3.0 kilometers radius
* Zone 2 (Mid): 3.1 to 6.0 kilometers radius
* Zone 3 (Far): 6.1 to 10.0 kilometers radius

*Deliveries requested outside the 10.0-kilometer radius are not accepted by our internal fleet but may be ordered via partner platforms (e.g., Zomato, Swiggy) if within their respective service boundaries.*

2. DELIVERY CHARGES
-------------------
Delivery charges are structured by zone to cover driver fuel and maintenance:
* Zone 1 (0.0 - 3.0 km):
  - Delivery Fee: ₹49
  - Free Delivery: For orders over ₹499 (subtotal before taxes and fees)
* Zone 2 (3.1 - 6.0 km):
  - Delivery Fee: ₹79
  - Free Delivery: For orders over ₹699 (subtotal before taxes and fees)
* Zone 3 (6.1 - 10.0 km):
  - Delivery Fee: ₹99
  - Free Delivery: For orders over ₹999 (subtotal before taxes and fees)

*Minimum Order Subtotal for Delivery: ₹250 (excluding taxes and delivery fee).*

3. ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIMES (EDT)
---------------------------------
Delivery times vary depending on order volume, traffic, weather, and distance:
* Zone 1 (0.0 - 3.0 km):
  - Standard Hours: 30 - 40 minutes
  - Peak Hours (Fri-Sat, 5:30 PM - 8:30 PM): 45 - 55 minutes
* Zone 2 (3.1 - 6.0 km):
  - Standard Hours: 40 - 50 minutes
  - Peak Hours (Fri-Sat, 5:30 PM - 8:30 PM): 55 - 65 minutes
* Zone 3 (6.1 - 10.0 km):
  - Standard Hours: 50 - 60 minutes
  - Peak Hours (Fri-Sat, 5:30 PM - 8:30 PM): 65 - 75 minutes

*Weather Advisory: During heavy rain, waterlogging, or extreme weather, add an additional 15-20 minutes to all zones for driver safety.*
