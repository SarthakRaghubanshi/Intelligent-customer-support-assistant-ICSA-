==================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ): PIZZA PARADISE
==================================================

Q1: What makes Pizza Paradise's crust special?
A1: Our signature crust is made from a natural 48-hour cold-fermented sourdough starter using organic Italian Tipo 00 flour. We hand-stretch each dough ball and bake it in our 900°F wood-fired brick oven for 90 to 120 seconds, giving it a blistered, chewy, and crispy texture.

Q2: Do you offer gluten-free crust options?
A2: Yes! We offer a 10" Personal-sized gluten-free crust made from rice and tapioca flour for an additional ₹99. 

Q3: How do you handle cross-contamination for food allergies?
A3: We take food safety very seriously. We have a dedicated prep station and separate clean utensils for building gluten-free and vegan pizzas. However, because our kitchen is a high-gluten and dairy environment, we cannot guarantee a 100% trace-free environment.

Q4: Do you have vegan cheese and meat options?
A4: Yes! We offer plant-based dairy-free vegan mozzarella cheese (available as a substitute for ₹59) and house-made vegan Italian sausage crumbles (made from soy and pea protein).

Q5: Which of your pizzas are fully vegan?
A5: We have three designated vegan pizzas: the Vegan Garden Party, Spicy Vegan Sausage, and Vegan Avocado Dream. You can also customize any regular pizza with our vegan cheese substitution.

Q6: What are your operating hours?
A6: We are open Monday through Thursday from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Friday and Saturday from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Sunday from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Our kitchen stops accepting new orders 30 minutes before closing time.

Q7: What is your delivery radius?
A7: We deliver within a 10.0-kilometer radius from our location at Plot 15, Block K, Sector 18, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301, India. 

Q8: How much do you charge for delivery?
A8: Delivery fees are based on your distance from the restaurant: Zone 1 (0-3 km) is ₹49 (free over ₹499), Zone 2 (3.1-6 km) is ₹79 (free over ₹699), and Zone 3 (6.1-10 km) is ₹99 (free over ₹999). The minimum subtotal for delivery is ₹250.

Q9: How long will my delivery take?
A9: For Zone 1, delivery is typically 30-40 minutes (45-55 mins during peak hours). For Zone 2, it is 40-50 minutes (55-65 mins peak). For Zone 3, it is 50-60 minutes (65-75 mins peak). Peak hours are Friday and Saturday from 5:30 PM to 8:30 PM.

Q10: Do you offer curbside pickup? How does it work?
A10: Yes! Park in one of our 4 designated curbside spots in front of the store, then click the link in your "Order Ready" SMS or call us at +91 120 4927200. Let us know your car's details and spot number, and a staff member will bring the food to your vehicle.
*Note: For online prepaid orders exceeding ₹2000, we require showing matching ID at pickup.*

Q11: Can I pay with cash on delivery (COD)?
A11: Yes, we accept Cash on Delivery for orders under ₹1500. For security reasons, our delivery drivers do not carry more than ₹500 in change, so please have close to exact change ready. Orders over ₹1500 must be prepaid online.

Q12: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A12: You can add items to your order until it goes into the oven. You can remove or change items (like toppings or crust) only within 5 minutes of placing your order. After 5 minutes, preparation starts, and the order is locked.

Q13: What is your cancellation policy?
A13: You can cancel for a full refund within 5 minutes of placing your order. Cancellations after 5 minutes incur a 50% cancellation fee to cover the cost of prepared ingredients. 

Q14: What should I do if my order is late?
A14: For Zone 1, we guarantee delivery within 45 minutes. If it takes longer, you are eligible for a ₹250 digital voucher. For Zones 2 & 3, if delivery is delayed by more than 20 minutes past the estimated window, we will refund your delivery fee (₹79 or ₹99) and send a 15% discount code.

Q15: What if my pizza arrives cold or damaged?
A15: If your food is cold or damaged upon arrival, please contact us within 2 hours at support@pizzaparadise.com or via live chat. Provide your order number and a photo of the affected items to receive a full refund or a free replacement.

Q16: How long do refunds take to process?
A16: Credit/debit card refunds are approved immediately but take 3 to 5 business days to appear in your bank account. Digital wallets (Apple/Google Pay) take 24-48 hours. Store credit is issued instantly.

Q17: I ordered through Zomato/Swiggy and have a problem. Can you help me?
A17: For orders placed on third-party platforms, you must request assistance and refunds directly through their respective apps. Our staff cannot modify or refund third-party orders.

Q18: What counts as a catering order, and how do I place one?
A18: Any order with 6 or more pizzas or totaling over ₹3500 is considered a catering order. These must be placed at least 3 hours in advance. A 10% auto-gratuity fee is added to support the kitchen team.

Q19: Can I buy gift cards or sign up for a loyalty program?
A19: Yes! You can purchase digital gift cards and join our Loyalty Program directly on pizzaparadise.com. Every ₹10 spent earns you 1 point, and 100 points can be redeemed for a free Personal pizza or a ₹250 credit.

Q20: Do you serve beer, wine, or other alcohol?
A20: No, Pizza Paradise does not serve or deliver alcoholic beverages. We offer soft drinks, San Pellegrino sparkling water, house-brewed iced tea, and organic lemonade.

Q21: Can I apply multiple promo codes to my order?
A21: No. Only one promotional code or discount coupon can be applied per order. Coupons cannot be stacked or combined with other active deals.

Q22: What happens if I don't pick up my order?
A22: Pickup orders are held in our heated cabinets for a maximum of 2 hours. If uncollected after 2 hours, the food is discarded for safety reasons, and you will be charged the full amount. No refunds are provided for uncollected orders.
