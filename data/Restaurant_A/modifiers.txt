=========================================
MODIFIERS & CUSTOMIZATION: PIZZA PARADISE
=========================================

1. CRUST OPTIONS
----------------
Customers can choose from the following crusts for any 10", 12", or 16" pizza:
* Classic Sourdough (Default)
  - Description: Our signature 48-hour cold-fermented dough made with Italian Tipo 00 flour. Creates a blistered, chewy, and crispy Neapolitan-style crust.
  - Price: Included in base pizza price.
* Gluten-Free Crust (Only available in 10" Personal size)
  - Description: Rice-and-tapioca-based dough prepared in a designated gluten-free zone to prevent cross-contamination. Crisps up beautifully.
  - Price: +₹99
* Stuffed Crust (Available in 12" Medium and 16" Large sizes)
  - Description: Our classic sourdough crust rolled and stuffed with a premium string mozzarella cheese, brushed with garlic butter.
  - Price: 12" Medium: +₹119 | 16" Large: +₹149
* Thin Crust (Available in all sizes)
  - Description: A Roman-style ultra-thin, cracker-crisp crust made with high-protein wheat flour.
  - Price: Included in base pizza price.

4. SPICE LEVEL OPTIONS
----------------------
Applied to the sauce base or sprinkled on top. No extra charge.
* Mild (No heat, standard kid-friendly flavor)
* Medium (A subtle kick, includes a light pinch of red chili flakes in the sauce)
* Hot (For spice enthusiasts, includes red chili flakes and sliced pickled jalapeños)
* Inferno (Extremely spicy, includes ghost pepper flakes and chopped fresh habanero peppers. *Caution: very hot!*)
