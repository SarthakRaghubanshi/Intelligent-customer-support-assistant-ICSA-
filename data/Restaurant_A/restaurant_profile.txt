Brand & Quality Promise:
------------------------
At Pizza Paradise, we are committed to elevating the pizza-ordering experience. Our hallmark is our signature crust, crafted from a natural 48-hour cold-fermented sourdough starter made with organic Italian Tipo 00 flour. Each pizza is hand-stretched and cooked in our custom wood-fired brick oven at a blistering 900°F, taking only 90 to 120 seconds to reach a blistered, chewy, and crispy perfection. We source our San Marzano tomatoes directly from Italy, and our fresh mozzarella cheese is delivered daily from local artisan dairies. We prioritize dietary inclusion, maintaining a completely separate preparation station and utensils for gluten-free and plant-based (vegan) items to ensure safety for customers with allergies and preferences.
