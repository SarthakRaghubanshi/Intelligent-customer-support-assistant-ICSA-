=========================================
PICKUP SETTINGS: PIZZA PARADISE
=========================================

1. PICKUP HOURS & TIMINGS
-------------------------
Pickup is available during standard operating hours. Please note that the kitchen stops taking new orders 30 minutes before closing:
* Monday - Thursday: 11:00 AM - 9:30 PM (Last pickup order accepted at 9:30 PM, pickup window closes at 10:00 PM)
* Friday - Saturday: 11:00 AM - 10:30 PM (Last pickup order accepted at 10:30 PM, pickup window closes at 11:00 PM)
* Sunday: 12:00 PM - 8:30 PM (Last pickup order accepted at 8:30 PM, pickup window closes at 9:00 PM)

*Average Preparation Time for Pickup:*
- Small/Medium Orders (1-2 pizzas/items): 15 - 20 minutes
- Large Orders (3-5 pizzas/items): 25 - 35 minutes
- Catering/Massive Orders (6+ items): 45 - 60 minutes (We recommend scheduling catering orders at least 3 hours in advance)

2. PICKUP CHANNELS & DESIGNATED AREAS
-------------------------------------
We offer two options for picking up orders:
* In-Store Counter Pickup: Walk into the lobby and head directly to the counter marked "Mobile & Online Pickups".
* Curbside Pickup: Park in one of our 4 designated curbside parking spots marked "Pizza Paradise Reserved Pickup" located directly in front of the main entrance.

3. PICKUP INSTRUCTIONS FOR CUSTOMERS
------------------------------------
* Step 1: Place your order via our app, website, or phone and select "Pickup". You will receive an SMS and email notification when your order goes into the oven, and another when it is boxed and ready.
* Step 2: Ensure you have your 4-digit order number (e.g., #1042) and the name on the order ready.
* Step 3:
  - For In-Store: Walk up to the pickup counter. Present your order number to the cashier. Your food is stored in our heated holding cabinet at 145°F to ensure it remains piping hot.
  - For Curbside: Once parked in a designated curbside spot, open the link in your "Order Ready" SMS or call +91 120 4927200. Let us know your car's make, model, color, and spot number. A team member will bring the food out to your vehicle and place it in your backseat or trunk for a completely contactless experience.
* Security Note: For orders exceeding ₹2000 paid online, we require the customer to show a valid ID matching the credit card used for payment at the time of pickup.
